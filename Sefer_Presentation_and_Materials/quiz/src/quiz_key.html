Marking: (a) 3 pts per row + 3 pts for the 2/3 rule; (b) 5 + 5.

Question 2 · NPC leg in the zero state25 pts

(a) Path: output a → S3 → node between S3 and S4 → D6 → midpoint O. Conducting devices: S3 and D6.

(b) Current flows from the output into O: the leg pushes current into the midpoint (current drawn from O is iO = ia < 0).

(c) S1 blocks 400 V (P to node X, which sits at O through S2) and S4 blocks 400 V (node Y at O to N). S2, S3, D5, D6 see ≈ 0 V.

(d) Going P → N directly switches both complementary pairs at once: the output jumps by the full 800 V (twice the dv/dt and EMI of a normal step), and during the transient the series devices S1–S2 or S3–S4 must share 800 V with no clamp guaranteeing equal sharing → one device can be overvoltaged. Nothing is saved: switching loss scales with the voltage step. Always pass through O.

Marking: (a) path 4 + devices 3; (b) 5; (c) 3 + 3; (d) step size 3, voltage sharing 3, conclusion 1.
