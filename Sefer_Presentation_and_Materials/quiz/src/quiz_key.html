Question 2 · NPC leg in the zero state25 pts

(a) Path: output a → S3 → node between S3 and S4 → D6 → midpoint O. Conducting devices: S3 and D6.

(b) Current flows from the output into O: the leg pushes current into the midpoint (current drawn from O is iO = ia < 0).

(c) S1 blocks 400 V (P to node X, which sits at O through S2) and S4 blocks 400 V (node Y at O to N). S2, S3, D5, D6 see ≈ 0 V.

(d) Going P → N directly switches both complementary pairs at once: the output jumps by the full 800 V (twice the dv/dt and EMI of a normal step), and during the transient the series devices S1–S2 or S3–S4 must share 800 V with no clamp guaranteeing equal sharing → one device can be overvoltaged. Nothing is saved: switching loss scales with the voltage step. Always pass through O.

Marking: (a) path 4 + devices 3; (b) 5; (c) 3 + 3; (d) step size 3, voltage sharing 3, conclusion 1.

Question 3 · DC-bus utilization and min-max injection25 pts

(a) V1,pk = 380 · √2 / √3 = 310.3 V;   m = 310.3 / 300 = 1.034.

(b) Sine PWM: no — m > 1, the references exceed the carriers (overmodulation, low-order harmonics). Min-max: yes — 1.034 < 2/√3 = 1.155.

(c) max = 0.98, min = −0.73 → r0 = −(0.98 − 0.73)/2 = −0.125;   r′ = (0.855, −0.375, −0.855).   r′a − r′b = 1.23 = ra − rb: the common offset cancels in every line-to-line difference.

(d) VLL,max = √3 · (2/√3)(Vdc/2) / √2 = Vdc/√2 = 424 V (sine PWM: 367 V).

Marking: (a) 6; (b) 2.5 + 2.5; (c) r0 3, r′ 3, check 3; (d) 5.

Question 4 · Neutral-point balancing25 pts

(a) ⟨iO⟩ = Σ(1 − |rx + r0|) ix. For a small offset that does not change the sign of rx: |rx + r0| = |rx| + sign(rx) r0, so Δ⟨iO⟩ = −r0 Σ sign(rx) ix.

(b) Averaged over a period, Σ sign(rx) ix = (6/π) Î cos φ. At φ = 90° this is ≈ 0: the offset no longer changes the average midpoint current, so ΔV is not restored (only the 3f1 ripple remains). Low power factor needs another method (redundant-vector selection, larger capacitors).

(c) The sensitivity −(6/π) Î cos φ changes sign with the power direction. In rectifier mode (cos φ < 0) the loop tuned as negative feedback becomes positive feedback: ΔV runs away until the overvoltage protection trips. Fix: r0 = sign(P) · K · ΔV (or a current-direction-aware state selection), and test both power directions.

Marking: (a) 8; (b) prediction 3, explanation via cos φ 5; (c) sign change 4, consequence 2, fix 3.
