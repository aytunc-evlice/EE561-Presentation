  1. For the positive half-cycle, state which devices conduct in states P and O, and identify the commutating pair. [5]

Solution 1 · Device stress in a three-level NPC leg25 pts

(a) Positive half-cycle (ia > 0): P → S1 + S2; O → D5 + S2. The commutating pair is S1 ↔ D5 (short loop C1–S1–D5); S2 stays on and conducts the whole half-cycle. [5]

(d) At m = 0.2: S1 = 6.3 + 20.4 = 26.7 W, D5 = 32.1 + 4.9 = 37.0 W, S2 = 38.9 W (unchanged). The zero-state duty 1 − m sin θ grows, so the inner switch and the clamp diode now carry most of the current; D5 overtakes S1. In rectifier operation the commutation moves to S3 ↔ D1, so the inner switches both conduct and switch; in an ANPC leg the zero state can use the active clamps (OU/OL paths) so that S5/S6 take the commutation and the loss is redistributed. (b) vaO = −Vdc/2 + S1(Vdc − Vfc) + S2Vfc,   ifc = (S1 − S2) ia. Averaging: ⟨vaO⟩ = −Vdc/2 + d1(Vdc − Vfc) + d2Vfc,   ⟨ifc⟩ = (d1 − d2) ia. Phase-shifted carriers with one reference give d1 = d2 → ⟨ifc⟩ = 0 and ⟨vaO⟩ = (2d − 1)Vdc/2. [6]

(c) ΔVfc,pp ≤ Î(Ts/2)/Cfc → Cfc ≥ Î/(2fswΔV) = 60/(2 · 20 000 · 10) = 150 µF (voltage rating ≥ 500 V plus margin). [6]

(d) Vfc is low → we need ifc = (S1 − S2)ia > 0. With ia < 0 this requires S1 − S2 = −1: state 01, ifc = +30 A. It produces vaO = −500 + 470 = −30 V instead of 0. [4]

(e) With Vfc = 0, state 11 places Vdc − Vfc = 1000 V across S1′, a device rated for about Vdc/2: it would fail. A pre-charge circuit or a controlled start-up sequence brings Vfc to 500 V first. [3]
