# Sefer İdacı — Multilevel converters: building the levels

EE561 Advanced Static Power Conversion, METU, Fall 2026. Part of the multilevel-converter module presented by
Sefer İdacı, Mustafa Aytunç Evlice and Elif Topaloğlu Kurmaç.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`Interactive_Deck/`](Interactive_Deck/) | The talk: an offline HTML deck with live simulations (19 slides + References, about 40 minutes, 6 backup slides). Open `Interactive_Deck/index.html` in Chrome or Edge; keys and outline in its [README](Interactive_Deck/README.md). |
| [`quiz/`](quiz/) | Printed 15-minute quiz, answer key and potential final questions (PDFs), with their HTML sources in `quiz/src/` (rebuild with `build_pdfs.ps1`). |

Topics: why multilevel (device physics, switching losses, real devices), NPC, T-type and ANPC legs, the flying capacitor,
the 5-level ANPC, device stress, a 1500 V design example and topology selection.
