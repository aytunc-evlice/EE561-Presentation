A three-phase, 3-level NPC converter must supply 380 V line-to-line RMS at 50 Hz to a three-wire grid from a 600 V DC link.

  1. Calculate the required modulation index m = V1,pk / (Vdc/2). [6]

Question 2 · Neutral-point balancingsimulation what-if and critique25 pts

In a 3-level NPC with carrier PWM, a common offset r0 is added to all three references to balance the split DC link (ΔV = vC1 − vC2).

  1. Starting from the relation for ⟨iO⟩ below, show how a small r0 changes the average midpoint current. [8]
  2. In the lecture simulation the loop restores ΔV within a few cycles at φ = 0°. Predict what happens at φ = 90° (pure reactive current) and explain. [8]
  3. A team implements r0 = +K·ΔV, tunes it on an inverter test bench, then ships the converter as an active rectifier. Critique this design and propose a fix. [9]

Useful relations

  • m = V1,pk / (Vdc/2); linear range m ≤ 1 (sine PWM), m ≤ 2/√3 ≈ 1.155 (min-max injection).   VLL,rms = √3 · V1,pk / √2.
  • Min-max offset: r0 = −[max(ra, rb, rc) + min(ra, rb, rc)] / 2,   r′x = rx + r0.
  • NPC with carrier PWM: ⟨iO⟩ = Σx (1 − |rx|) ix (current drawn from O),   C dΔV/dt = iO.
