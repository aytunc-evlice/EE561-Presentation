# M. Aytunç Evlice — Multilevel converters: running the levels

EE561 Advanced Static Power Conversion, METU, Fall 2026. Part of the multilevel-converter module presented by
Sefer İdacı, Mustafa Aytunç Evlice and Elif Topaloğlu Kurmaç.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`Interactive_Deck/`](Interactive_Deck/) | Slides for this part: an offline HTML deck with live simulations (20 slides, about 45 minutes). Open `Interactive_Deck/index.html` in Chrome or Edge; keys and outline in its [README](Interactive_Deck/README.md). |
| [`quiz/`](quiz/) | Quiz (one page, two questions), answer key and potential final questions on modulation and balancing (PDFs), with their HTML sources in `quiz/src/` (rebuild with `build_pdfs.ps1`). |

Topics: sine-triangle PWM, carrier-based multilevel PWM, min-max injection, space vectors and dwell times,
neutral-point and flying-capacitor balancing, the averaged model and dq current control.

These slides were Part II of the original joint deck (commit `d01966a`, 2 November 2026). When the module was split
between three presenters they were taken out of Sefer’s deck, and they are kept here unchanged as the starting point
for this part. Edit or replace them freely.
