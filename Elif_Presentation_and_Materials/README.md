# Elif Topaloğlu — Cascaded H-bridge multilevel inverters

EE561 Advanced Static Power Conversion, METU, Fall 2026. Part of the multilevel-inverter module presented by
Sefer İdacı, Mustafa Aytunç Evlice and Elif Topaloğlu Kurmaç.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`Interactive_Deck/`](Interactive_Deck/) | The talk: a single-file offline HTML deck with live simulations (21 slides including the title and References). Open `Interactive_Deck/index.html` in Chrome or Edge; keys and outline in its [README](Interactive_Deck/README.md). |

Topics: why and when CHB multilevel inverters are used and their advantages and disadvantages, the single-phase H-bridge cell, bipolar and unipolar PWM, the CHB with equal and unequal DC voltages and its
switching states, phase-shifted and level-shifted carrier PWM and their comparison, staircase modulation (selective
harmonic elimination). Main reference: Wu and Narimani, Chapter 7.

Order in the module: Sefer (building the levels) → Aytunç (modulate, balance, control) → this talk. It applies Aytunç’s
phase-shifted and level-shifted carriers cell by cell.

Online (GitHub Pages): <https://aytunc-evlice.github.io/EE561-Presentation/Elif_Presentation_and_Materials/Interactive_Deck/>
